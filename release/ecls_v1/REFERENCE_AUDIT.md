## Submission format

The prepared review PDF follows that reading format; its page count is not a typeset journal-page estimate. No journal acceptance or suitability guarantee is inferred.

The rewritten main narrative is restricted to the ECLS and candidate-ranking evidence. The supplement carries numerical methods and sensitivity details rather than unrelated platform studies. AI-assisted editorial work is disclosed for author review. References and author declarations must be checked in the final approved version.
